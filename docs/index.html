An AI copilot that operates the canvas

Not a chat bolted to the side. The agent receives your canvas state — synced every 80 ms — and acts on it through 12 frontend tools:

  • createChatNode
  • createBranchFromNode
  • mergeChatNodes
  • deleteChatNode
  • updateChatNode
  • focusChatNode
  • appendNodeMessage
